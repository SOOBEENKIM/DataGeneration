# Post-fit explanatory diagnostics, before inspecting new generation quality

The four fitted heads are frozen. No new fitting, selection, or hyperparameter changes.
Use optimization and internal-validation actual prefixes only, with the registered
noise seeds. Break the conditional normal timing error into the already motivated
1–49/50–199/200–499/500–1023/1024–2047/2048+ intervals and the optimization-defined
clock bands. Record event-weighted and equal-customer W1, sample counts, mean offset,
and source/generated clock discrepancy in the main free-generation report.

This diagnoses which customer exposures and positions account for changes. It is
post-fit descriptive analysis, not an additional preregistered primary endpoint or
proof that a mechanism is causal. Do not alter the models based on it. In particular,
do not call a failure under actual internal prefixes purely autoregressive drift.
Plot the frozen learned correction functions as standalone PNG/PDF artifacts.
