# Fixed-model generation repeats v1

Registered 2026-09-20 before implementation and new scientific generation.

## Question and fixed scope
Does Ecal's observed native generation difference from Ucal persist after averaging fresh generation randomness? This evaluates existing models; no neural training, calibration fitting, checkpoint selection, architecture changes, metric changes, or data regeneration are allowed. Earlier results and failed screens remain unchanged.

Use U, Ucal, E, Ecal at all four prevalences (.05/.10/.25/.50), both kappa conditions (0/1), and all five existing paired training trials. Exactly five NEW generation seeds per trial, with `2026092100 + 100*trial + repeat` for repeat 0–4. The same seed is shared across four models and prevalence/kappa cells within a trial/repeat; different trials use disjoint seeds. Original seeds 20261201–20261205 are reference-only and excluded from the new primary average. No early stopping, selecting successful repeats, or adding repeats after inspecting results.

160 frozen model states × five fresh tapes = 800 generated datasets, each 2,048 entities (1,638,400 sequences). Freeze each trial/cell's ORIGINAL train-derived entity/context/length plan, entity order, batch size 256, sampling implementation and float32/TF32 settings. Only torch generation randomness changes. In particular do not change the plan's NumPy sampling seed when changing generation seeds. Fully generate gap, mark and numeric value recursively, starting from the first event. Retain all generated arrays and scalar metrics. Do not train on oracle labels or evaluation data; test stays untouched.

## Endpoints and estimands
Primary: native active-context gap-bin repeat-curve L1 in kappa=1, Ecal minus Ucal. Reuse the full 13-metric generation suite, train-fitted bins/scales/mark groups, validation reference and all three evaluation groups. Secondary contrasts: Ucal−U, Ecal−E, E−U, Ecal−U; factorial interaction (Ecal−E)−(Ucal−U). Report both active and all null-cell generation scores. Previously evaluated conditional TV and null intervention response are fixed-model quantities; reuse with hashes and do NOT count them as new repeated observations.

Let d[j,r] be the paired metric difference for training trial j and fresh tape r. First average the five tapes WITHIN each trial, then compare the five trial means. Report all 25 paired values, five trial means, their SD, and the original descriptive 95% t interval (df=4). These are five training trials, not 25 independent trained models. The primary endpoint is mean of per-dataset errors; do not pool generated events across tapes and substitute the error of a larger dataset (nonlinear metrics differ).

Also report each trial's within-tape SD and the MC SE of the overall fixed-model mean: sqrt(sum_j s_j²/(J² R)), J=R=5. A conditional Monte Carlo t interval uses Satterthwaite df = (sum_j s_j²/R)² / sum_j[(s_j²/R)²/(R−1)]. This interval describes only fresh-generation uncertainty conditional on these five fixed models/plans and the reused validation data; it is NOT a population, training, or independent-data superiority interval. When all variances are zero, record zero MC SE and a degenerate conditional interval.

Descriptive variance components: mean within-tape variance W; sample variance B_obs of the five tape-averaged trial differences; residual between-trial estimate B_raw=B_obs−W/R, and B=max(0,B_raw). Show the unclipped value and truncation flag. The observed between-trial variation also includes different fixed generation plans tied to training trials; do not label it pure training variance. Report expected MC variance contribution W/R to trial means; do not use a noisy ratio to claim an exclusive cause. Fixed validation-sample uncertainty, finite-generation-size bias and variation across data-generation seeds are not estimated here.

## Decisions fixed before results
Keep the preceding exploratory consistency screen: negative grand mean and at least 4/5 negative tape-averaged trial effects at at least 3/4 prevalences. Its sign-reversed counterpart (positive mean, at least 4/5 positive trial effects, at least 3/4 prevalences) describes a persistent Ecal generation cost. Report both even if neither holds. The stricter joint improvement lead also requires nonpositive mean active MI-error and both reused null copy/repeat response contrasts at qualifying prevalences. No retrospective acceptable-cost margin, composite score, new success threshold, or confirmatory p-value.

Compare the original single-tape contrast with new tape averages and show each trial's fresh-tape range. Neither a failed consistency screen nor a confidence interval spanning zero proves equality, a purely random effect, or absence of a structural problem. A narrow MC interval away from zero can reject a 'just this generation draw' explanation for THESE frozen models/plans, while between-trial uncertainty may still prevent a broader claim. All four prevalences share underlying data and must not be counted as independent replications.

If an improvement repeats, next propose independent data/training seeds. If a generation cost repeats, retain E's conditional/null-response benefits separately and diagnose the joint generation/history distribution before a minimal targeted change. If mixed, state uncertainty and compare practical tradeoffs without automatically modifying architecture or searching seeds. Do not automatically launch another study.
