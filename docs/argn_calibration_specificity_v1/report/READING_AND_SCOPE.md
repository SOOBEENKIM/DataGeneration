# Reading and interpretation scope — 2026-10-01

Supplement after registration, before final results; no protocol or implementation change.

The three controls remove explicit personal-speed dependence ONLY FROM THE ADDITIVE CALIBRATION. The fixed neural history mixture still receives ARGN's strict-past representation and can encode personal behavior. A negative result therefore does not mean customer history is useless; it means this particular explicit speed interaction has not demonstrated additional value over calibrating the same history model more simply.

The normal→normal correction changes generated gaps, which change subsequent ARGN input and the strict-past median used in personal ratios. Fraud-onset gap laws are not directly corrected. The unchanged label/count schedule means this study cannot establish improved fraud incidence, customer allocation or transition learning. An onset-personal change is a population relation effect through generation, not proof of predicting factual fraud onset or its causal mechanism.

Position3 is the exact nested explicit-speed removal with tied coefficient pairs. Position6 uses the same coefficient count as personal calibration but has six position knots instead of three position knots crossed with two speed endpoints. Thus position6 distinguishes parameter budget from feature use, but it also changes position resolution. Both controls are needed for interpreting their different effects.

Primary source method sections rechecked during execution:

- [MoTPP2026 §4.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.21382v1): known THP backbone, zero-inflated mixture output and likelihood. Its mark/time factorization assumes conditional independence given shared history. This is useful design precedent, not a proof of our joint relations or an equivalent financial calibration.
- [MeLD2026 Methods and Discussion](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-74492-0): known VAE with latent diffusion, component/objective contrasts and longitudinal analysis. GRU-to-Transformer VAE replacement did not clearly improve results. Some competing models retained specific tail or short-event advantages. The paper caps visits at120; applying its official code to thousands of Sparkov transactions is not a completed reproduction or fair comparator yet.

These observations support testing one concrete effect and reporting costs. They do not grant novelty, theoretical stability, financial superiority or publication certainty. Full closest-method and untouched-data comparisons remain necessary if the bounded effect survives.
