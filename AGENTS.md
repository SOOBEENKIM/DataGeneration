# Research working agreement

- Read `docs/RESEARCH_DECISIONS.md` before proposing model changes, launching a new research experiment, or stating a contribution. The user's standing instruction is to connect current primary literature to those decisions proactively.
- Before a new model change, record the closest prior method, exact overlap, evidence for the remaining problem, and the experiment that distinguishes the proposed change. Recheck relevant current literature when the proposed mechanism changes or an earlier search is insufficient. A bibliography alone is not this comparison.
- Separate baseline reproduction, preprocessing/configuration fixes, diagnostic controls, and potential methodological contributions. A successful fix or an improvement over ARGN alone does not establish novelty or superiority to current methods.
- If the closest prior work or relevant comparison is unresolved, report that directly. Continue useful diagnosis and literature work under existing authorization; do not expand an ungrounded candidate into large model training or claim its novelty.
- Preserve frozen protocols, original outputs, unsuccessful fits, and provenance. Add dated amendments instead of retroactively changing registered experimental conditions. Do not silently alter running fits or read held-out test events for development.
- Keep the user informed in concise Korean. Do not ask the user to repeat these requirements or reapprove already-authorized work. Do not spawn subagents without explicit authorization.
